## New

- A fresh coat of paint. The feedback window and this very card get a new look: soft continuous corners with a recessed panel inside.
- Discord keeps up when you watch elsewhere. Sending a stream to VLC, IINA, or mpv no longer flips your Discord status to Paused — it keeps showing what you're watching.

## Improvements

- Audio now starts in English when the file has multiple languages, and never picks a track your system can't decode.

## Fixes

- The app remembers exactly how you left the window — size, position, and all.
- Streams from some sources refused to play because of how they announce themselves. Sorted.
