## New

- Sort the source list your way. New **Default / Quality / Size** tabs in the source picker let you order streams by highest quality or biggest file. Your pick is remembered.
- A **Developer** switch in Settings. Turn it on to open DevTools with a shortcut and peek under the hood, even in the installed app.

## Fixes

- The fullscreen button and picture-in-picture actually work now. PiP no longer doubles up the video, opens while paused, and stays in sync with play and pause.
- Streams from some sources were silently blocked from playing. They play now.
