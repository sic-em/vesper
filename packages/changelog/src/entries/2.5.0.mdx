## New

- Anime4K upscaling for anime. Low-resolution anime looks soft on a modern display, so the player can now sharpen and double it in real time on your GPU. Flip it on from the player's right-click menu or Settings → Playback; it remembers your choice and turns itself on for anime only. Three presets cover weak to strong GPUs, it steps itself down (and tells you) if playback starts dropping frames, and it stays out of the way on HDR or already-sharp sources. <By u="sic-em" pr={54} />
