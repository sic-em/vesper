## New

- Binge mode. When the credits roll, a card counts down and rolls straight into the next episode — cancel it with a click, or turn it off entirely in **Settings → Playback**. The next episode is fetched while the card is up, so there's no wait when it flips over. <By u="userlaws" pr={10} />
- Still watching? After a few unattended episodes, Vesper asks before advancing instead of playing to an empty couch. Any keypress or click keeps the binge going. <By u="userlaws" pr={10} />
- Picture-in-picture can now tuck the main window away. A **Minimize Vesper** button on the placeholder, plus an optional setting that minimizes on PiP open and restores the window exactly as it was on close. <By u="userlaws" pr={10} />

## Fixes

- The next and previous episode buttons actually play the episode now, instead of dumping you on the show page — and they cross season boundaries instead of dead-ending at a finale. <By u="userlaws" pr={10} />
- Skipping ahead no longer marks the next episode as watched before it even starts, or lands you two episodes forward. <By u="userlaws" pr={10} />
- Some sources served a "torrent is being downloaded" clip instead of the real episode. Vesper now spots the impostor and moves on to the next source. <By u="userlaws" pr={10} />
- Your volume carries over from episode to episode instead of resetting to full blast. <By u="userlaws" pr={10} />
- Subtitles and audio language stick through a binge — they used to silently drop off from episode two onward. <By u="userlaws" pr={10} />
