## New

- Send what you're watching to another player. If you have VLC, IINA, or mpv installed, a new button in the playback controls hands the stream over and picks up right where you left off.

## Improvements

- A quieter look across the app: softer corners and lighter text.
- Hovering the timeline now shows a clean timestamp instead of video thumbnails. The thumbnails caused stutter during playback, so they're gone.

## Fixes

- Streams load again. A hiccup on our end stopped playback from starting at all.
