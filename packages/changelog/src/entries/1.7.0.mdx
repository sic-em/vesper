## New

- A fresh coat of paint, everywhere. Every modal, menu, and popover — sources, subtitles, audio, notifications, lists, feedback, and more — got a new look: soft continuous corners with a recessed panel inside. <By u="sic-em" pr={8} /> <By u="sic-em" pr={9} />
- A new typeface. The whole app now renders in Inter on every platform, so Vesper looks the same on Windows as it does on a Mac. <By u="sic-em" pr={9} />
- Sort the source list your way. New **Default / Quality / Size** tabs in the source picker order streams by highest quality or biggest file, and your pick is remembered. <By u="userlaws" pr={3} />
- Discord keeps up when you watch elsewhere. Sending a stream to VLC, IINA, or mpv no longer flips your Discord status to Paused — it keeps showing what you're watching. <By u="sic-em" pr={8} />
- A **Developer** switch in Settings. Turn it on to open DevTools with a shortcut and peek under the hood, even in the installed app. <By u="sic-em" pr={2} />
- Credit where it's due. Changelog entries now show who built each change, with a link to the pull request — like the little chips on this very page. <By u="sic-em" pr={9} />

## Improvements

- Audio now starts in English when the file has multiple languages, and never picks a track your system can't decode. <By u="userlaws" pr={5} />

## Fixes

- The fullscreen button and picture-in-picture actually work now, and PiP stays in sync with play and pause. <By u="userlaws" pr={1} />
- The app remembers exactly how you left the window — size, position, and all. <By u="userlaws" pr={7} />
- Streams from some sources refused to play because of how they announce themselves. Sorted. <By u="userlaws" pr={6} />
