## Removed

- Notifications are gone — the bell, the notifications page, and the chime. Friend requests now live entirely on the Friends page, same as before, just without the extra inbox. <By u="sic-em" pr={22} />
- List collaboration and share links are gone too. Shared lists, invites, and join links have been removed; your lists are yours alone now. Public lists still show on your profile, and friends can still save a copy of them. <By u="sic-em" pr={21} />

## Improvements

- The app is leaner for it — fewer background queries and a quieter top bar.
